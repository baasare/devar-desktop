THE DESKTOP COMPANION

From spoken reference
to ready-to-copy verse.

Devar supports the live media workflow without taking over the service.

01
≋

Choose the feed

Select a microphone or connected soundboard input. Devar listens only after the operator starts it.

02
⌁

Catch the moment

A rolling transcript helps Devar recognize a spoken reference or match quoted scripture.

03
↗

Review and copy

Check the ranked passages, copy the reference or verse, and paste it into your presentation.

A QUIETER KIND OF AI

The sermon stays
with your team.

Speech recognition and scripture search run on the Mac. Devar keeps only a short transcript window in memory while listening; it does not save recordings or full transcripts.

01Nothing to sign intoNo account or analytics in the companion.
02Models downloaded onceSetup fetches model files from their upstream sources.
03Copy is your choiceNo automatic typing, clipboard writes, or slide changes.
